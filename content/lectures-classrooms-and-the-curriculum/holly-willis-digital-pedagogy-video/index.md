This space was created for the Hacking the Academy, a week-long crowdsourced book project growing out of THATcamp, George Mason University, May 21-28, 2010. 


This particular entry is culled from the recent HASTAC conference, April 15-17, 2010 which was entirely virtual. These videos, by Holly Willis and Virginia Kuhn, served as one of the keynote addresses as Willis and Kuhn live-blogged them, VJ style during the conference. The 11 videos are grouped here, and consist of one short introduction by Willis and Kuhn, and then five clips each (for a total of ten 2-3 minute-long clips) done individually and centered on five themes surrounding digital pedagogy.
